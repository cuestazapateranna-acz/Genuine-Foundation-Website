Genuine Cup 2026

50 Countries,
50 Teams —
Inclusion in Motion.

July 27 – August 1, 2026  ·  Houston, Texas

Six days of professional team sport built around and for the IDD community. Where inclusion doesn't just get talked about — it gets played out on the field, every single day.

About the Event
The Event

A real competition. Real athletes. Real dignity.

The Genuine Cup is the heartbeat of everything the Genuine Foundation builds toward. A multi-day sporting competition that brings athletes with intellectual and developmental disabilities into a real, professional team sport environment — with the structure, the visibility, and the dignity that every athlete deserves.

This is not a charitable program. It is a real competition. And the athletes at its center deserve to be treated that way.

Who gets involved

Every role matters. Every person belongs.

The Genuine Cup is built by and for an entire community — athletes, volunteers, sponsors, families, and fans who show up because inclusion is the point.

Athletes with IDD

Competing at the center of it all — not as a side event, not as an afterthought, but as the whole point. Real competition, real teammates, real recognition.

Volunteers

From Genuine Connect and the broader community, supporting teams throughout the week — putting the summit's ideas into action on the field.

Sponsors & Partners

Whose presence means something beyond a logo on a banner. Organizations demonstrating meaningful commitment to IDD inclusion in one of its most visible, tangible forms.

Families & Community Members

Who show up to cheer, connect, and witness something real — the moment when the people they love are seen for exactly who they are.

How to get involved

There's a role here for everyone.

You can support the Genuine Cup as a volunteer, a sponsor, or simply by being there. Every role matters.

Organizations looking to demonstrate meaningful commitment to IDD inclusion will find the Genuine Cup to be one of the most visible, tangible ways to do it.
