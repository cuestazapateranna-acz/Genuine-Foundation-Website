Genuine Connect 2026

Shape How the
Next Generation Leads.

July 25–26, 2026  ·  Rice University  ·  Houston, Texas

A two-day summit where students, companies, policy makers, and advocates come together to advance inclusion and build real pathways to employment for the IDD community.

Register Interest → About the Summit
The Summit

Where knowledge becomes action.

Approximately 300 students from across the country — spanning engineering, business, psychology, human resources, and more — gather at Rice University for two fully immersive days. Lodging and meals are covered.

Participants don't just attend. They present, engage with speakers, debate ideas, and challenge their own assumptions about inclusion, disability, and the future of work. Then they step onto the field.

The Program

Two days. Lasting change.

Every session is designed to translate understanding into action — and action into opportunity.

Top-tier panels

Speakers from UT, Rice, Best Buddies, Bank of America, HP, La Fageda, and Special Olympics share real stories and real strategies for advancing IDD inclusion in the workforce.

Student Poster Session

Selected students showcase research on IDD and neurodiversity, bridging academic knowledge and lived experience — and presenting it to people with the power to act on it.

Film Screening

Special premiere of the Genuine documentary, produced in partnership with BBC, followed by a facilitated discussion that connects the film's themes to the summit's work.

Genuine Cup Volunteer

Participants transition directly into volunteering at the Genuine Cup, putting the summit's ideas into action on the field — where inclusion becomes real, lived, and undeniable.

Who should be here

Built for people who want to build something better.

Anyone who believes the workforce of tomorrow needs to look and work differently — and wants to be part of building it.

Students

Across any discipline. If you're curious about inclusion, equity, and the future of work — this summit was made for you.

HR & Talent Professionals

Looking to move beyond intention. The summit provides frameworks, contacts, and case studies to act on inclusive hiring.

Policy Makers

Who need to see what's working at the ground level, and connect with organizations already creating systemic change.

Corporate Leaders & Advocates

Ready to move from awareness to accountability. Genuine Connect offers the context, relationships, and momentum to do it.
