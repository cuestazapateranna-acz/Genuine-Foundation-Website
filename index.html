The Genuine Foundation Mission
Why it matters

1 in 7 people live with an intellectual or developmental disability.

Most will never access meaningful employment — not because they can't contribute, but because the world hasn't made room for them. The Genuine Foundation exists to change that, through sport, education, and community built around real inclusion.

The employment gap is real

85% of adults with IDD who want to work are currently unemployed or underemployed — a number that hasn't meaningfully improved in decades.

Visibility drives change

When people with IDD are seen competing, presenting, and leading, perceptions shift. That shift opens doors that policy alone cannot.
