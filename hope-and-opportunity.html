Hope & Opportunity

An Evening
for Change.

June 11–13, 2026  ·  Soleil Houston  ·  Houston, Texas

Three extraordinary Michelin-starred evenings where culinary excellence and human purpose share the same table. Every seat supports a future where people with IDD are seen, employed, and celebrated.

Discover the Experience
The Experience

More than a dinner. A declaration.

Hope & Opportunity is an intimate dining series at Soleil Houston — a Michelin-recognized dining experience — across three carefully curated evenings in June. Guests are treated to a seven-course dinner designed not just to delight, but to connect. The conversation around the table matters as much as what's on it.

This is not a conventional fundraising gala. It is an evening designed to bring together people who care — and who want to do something meaningful together over a meal worth remembering.

The Program

Three evenings. One purpose.

Each evening is carefully designed to create connection — between guests, between purpose and pleasure, between the world as it is and the world as it should be.

June 11

Dinner Evening I

Seven-course Michelin-star dinner at Soleil Houston. An intimate evening of exceptional cuisine and meaningful conversation.

June 12

Dinner Evening II

Seven-course Michelin-star dinner at Soleil Houston. A second evening with a fresh perspective and a different gathering of guests committed to change.

June 13

Wine Tasting

An intimate curated wine experience at Soleil Houston. A closing evening celebrating discovery, taste, and the stories behind each pour.

Why it matters

A seat at the table for everyone.

Proceeds from Hope & Opportunity directly support the Genuine Foundation's mission — funding programs, partnerships, and infrastructure that create real pathways to employment for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

When you take a seat at the table, you help create space for others to do the same.

Real impact, directly funded

Every ticket purchased funds programs that connect neurodiverse talent with employers, educators, and communities ready to act.

A network that moves

The people around the table are potential partners, donors, advocates, and champions for the communities we serve.
